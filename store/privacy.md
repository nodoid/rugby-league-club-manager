# Privacy notice — Rugby League Club Manager

**Last updated: 5 November 2026**

Rugby League Club Manager collects no personal data.

## What the game stores

- One career save file, written into the app's own private container on the device.
- The restart of that save when you take charge of a new club.

That file never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

## What the game does not do

- No account, login, email address or profile.
- No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- No network requests of any kind. The game is fully offline.
- No access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data or location.
- No tracking across apps or websites.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Permissions

None are requested. The builds declare no Android permissions beyond the minimum the MonoGame
runtime needs to run, and no iOS permission usage strings.

## Contact

Open an issue at
`https://github.com/nodoid/rugby-league-club-manager/issues` and it will be answered there.

## App Store privacy answers

- Data Not Collected.
- Tracking: No.

## Google Play Data safety answers

- Data collected: none.
- Data shared: none.
- Data deletion: uninstalling the app deletes the save.
