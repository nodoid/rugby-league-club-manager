## Full description (4000 max)

**Take charge of a rugby league club and play the season your way.**

Rugby League Club Manager puts you in the coach's box and then on the pitch. Run one of 48
fictional clubs across four competitions in two countries, deal with contracts, injuries, sponsors
and a demanding board, and decide every week whether to watch the match unfold or pull on the boots
yourself.

BUILD A SQUAD
• 13-a-side lineups plus an interchange bench, with a "select best XVII" shortcut
• Player ratings, form, sharpness, morale, fitness and contracts
• Training with seven focuses, facility upgrades and a youth academy
• Injuries from a knock to season-ending, with automatic selection changes

RUN THE CLUB
• Balance, salary cap, wages, gate receipts, TV money, prize money and ticket pricing
• Transfer listings, free agents, bids and counter-offers, plus a mid-season window
• Main, sleeve, shorts, stadium and kit-supplier sponsors with win and trophy bonuses
• Board expectations, a news feed and an end-of-season summary

PLAY THE MATCH TWO WAYS
• 3D arcade match: control the ball carrier in attack and the nearest defender in defence. Drag the
  stick to run anywhere, then pass, kick, sprint and tackle with big touch controls
• Top-down simulation: every carry, kick, penalty, error and interchange resolved from attributes
  and tactics, with pause, speed controls and an instant result

A FULL RUGBY LEAGUE SEASON
• Four competitions: NRL, NRL Championship, Super League and The Championship
• Double round-robin seasons, seeded finals, promotion and relegation, and the Challenge Shield
  knockout cup for all 48 clubs
• Six-tackle sets, 10-metre retreats, conversions with power and wind, penalty goals, drop goals,
  40/20 kicks, goal-line drop-outs, sin bins, head injury assessments and golden point

PLAY ANYWHERE
• Designed for phones and tablets in landscape, with touch controls throughout
• One career save per device, written automatically after every week
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection

Every club, player, sponsor and competition is fictional. No real club badges, kits or sponsors are
used.
