# How to play

Rugby League Club Manager is a season-long club-management game with two ways to play each match.
Everything saves automatically as you go, so you can put it down and pick it up mid-season.

## Getting started

1. Launch the game and wait for the splash screen, then tap **New career**.
2. Pick a competition tab (NRL, NRL Championship, Super League or The Championship), then tap a
   club card to select it.
3. Tap **Take charge**. You land on the **Dashboard** for your club with the squad, finances and
   next fixture already filled in.

Your manager name is generated for you; tap **New** next to it on the club-selection screen for a
different one.

## The weekly loop

The navigation column on the left is the whole game:

| Tab | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Board expectations, last result, next fixture, news, season summary. |
| **Squad** | Pick your XVII and bench, sort by position or rating, check form, sharpness and fitness, offer contracts, transfer-list players. |
| **Tactics** | Compare formations and set the sliders: kick early, offloads, line speed, width, plus team style. |
| **Training** | Choose the week's focus, then upgrade training facilities and the youth academy. |
| **Transfers** | Bid for listed players and free agents, respond to offers for your own squad, follow the mid-season window. |
| **Club** | Finances, sponsor offers, ticket pricing, facility investment and the ledger. |
| **Competitions** | League table, fixtures and results for every division, plus the Challenge Shield cup. |

When you are ready, press **Matchday** at the bottom of the column (it shows your next opponent).
Advancing a week without a fixture is the **Advance** button in the same place.

## Matchday

The matchday screen shows both team sheets, the weather and the wind. You have three choices:

- **Play 3D match** — you take the ball yourself in an arcade third-person match.
- **Watch simulation** — the top-down animated simulation with speed controls and your tactics applied.
- **Quick result** — resolve the match instantly and go straight to the result screen.

### 3D controls

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Stick** (bottom-left) | Drag anywhere in the pad to run in that direction — your player turns to face it. The marker at their feet shows the way they are facing. |
| **PASS** | Send the ball to the nearest support runner. |
| **KICK** | Punt downfield. On a conversion, stop the power bar in the green band; a following wind helps, a headwind hurts. |
| **TACKLE** | While defending, close the gap and then tackle. |
| **RUN** | Hold to sprint (costs stamina). |
| **PAUSE** | Top-left; resume or leave the match. |

On a desktop keyboard: **WASD**/arrows to run, **Space** pass, **K** kick, **J** tackle, **Shift**
sprint, **P** pause, **Enter** to dismiss the how-to-play card.

Conversions follow the law: the kick is taken from a point on the line through where the try was
scored, ten metres back from the goal line, so the angle gets harder the wider the try was scored.

### Simulation

The 2D match runs itself. Use the speed control to fast-forward, pause at any time, or leave and
take the instant result. Everything — carries, kicks, penalties, sin bins, interchanges and golden
point — is resolved from player attributes, tactics and conditions.

## After the match

The result screen shows the score, scorers, match stats, man of the match and any injuries. Press
**Continue** to go back to the hub with the week already advanced.

## Season structure

Each division plays a double round-robin, then a seeded finals series for the top clubs. The
Challenge Shield is a knockout cup for all 48 clubs, one round every three weeks. At the end of the
season the bottom club in each top division is relegated and the top club of each second division
is promoted, with a play-off for the second promotion place.

## Saving

There is one career save per device and it is written after every week, transfer and team selection.
**Save** in the hub header forces one immediately; **Menu** returns to the title screen (saving first).
Starting a **New career** replaces the existing save once you take charge of a club.

## Tips

- Rotate players: fitness below about 70% costs you late in matches, and tired players get injured.
- Set tactics before big games — line speed and kick-early matter most against stronger packs.
- Keep cap space free: the mid-season transfer window shuts after round 8.
- Accept sponsor offers early in the season; they expire and the bonuses only count once you are
  signed up.
